Immunooncology (IO) drug development increasingly depends on understanding human specific immune mechanisms. However, these subtle, highly coordinated interactions simply cannot be replicated by traditional murine immune systems. Many next generation immunotherapies rely on receptors, signaling pathways, and effector functions that exist only in humans.
