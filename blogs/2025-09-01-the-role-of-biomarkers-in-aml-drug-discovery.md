The Urgency for Innovative AML Therapies Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) is an aggressive blood cancer with low survival rates, despite recent progress in targeted therapies. This highlights the urgent need for innovative drug discovery approaches. Biomarker-guided strategies and robust AML models are central to advancing therapeutic development.
