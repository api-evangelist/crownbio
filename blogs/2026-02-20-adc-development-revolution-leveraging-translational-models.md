In the rapidly evolving landscape of oncology drug discovery, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) represent one of the most promising therapeutic modalities. In this CrownCast episode, Benjamin Wilkin, Product Marketing Manager at Crown Bioscience, sits down with Davy Ouyang, SVP of Innovation at Crown Bioscience, to explore how advanced translational models are transforming ADC development and overcoming the unique challenges of this complex therapeutic class.
