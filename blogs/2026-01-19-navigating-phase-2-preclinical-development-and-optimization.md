Phase 2, of the drug discovery process, or preclinical development and optimization, is a critical stage in drug discovery. It bridges early lead identification with preparation for an Investigational New Drug (IND) application. Once a lead compound has been identified, researchers focus on evaluating its efficacy, safety, pharmacokinetics, and biomarker potential in relevant biological systems.
