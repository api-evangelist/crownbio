Why Tumor Heterogeneity Challenges Clinical Trials Tumor heterogeneity remains a major obstacle in clinical trials. Differences between tumors and even within a single tumor can drive drug resistance by altering treatment targets or shaping the tumor microenvironment (TME). These variations occur within tumors, across primary and metastatic sites, and change over the course of disease progression.
