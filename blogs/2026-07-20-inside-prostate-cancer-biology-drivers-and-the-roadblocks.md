Although many prostate tumors are detected while still localized, prostate cancer remains a major focus of oncology drug discovery because of its high incidence, clinical heterogeneity, and the complexity of treating the disease. In the United States, prostate cancer remains one of the most diagnosed cancers among men, with over 330,000 new cases and more than 36,000 deaths projected annually, despite significant advances in screening and treatment. 1
