Imagine standing on the brink of a breakthrough in cancer treatment, only to be halted by one daunting barrier: drug resistance. In this episode of CrownCast, Jonny McMichael, PhD, VP of Client Experience and Enablement at Crown Bioscience, alongside Enrico Pesenti, the company’s Executive Director of Client Engagement, delve into this critical issue. Their discussion highlights that drug resistance affects a staggering number of patients, transforming promising therapies into temporary solutions.
